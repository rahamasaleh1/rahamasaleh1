# Hi!

I am Rahama, I graduated from De Montfort University with a First Class degree in Applied Computing and I have worked in tech since 2018, starting on an IT service desk.

Most problems are not really technical, they come from a product that doesn't match how people work. That is why I want to build my career in product.

## Things I have built

**[MedVerify](https://github.com/rahamasaleh1/MedicineVerificationSystem)**: my dissertation project. It checks whether a medicine is genuine, counterfeit or expired. [Try the live app](https://rahamasaleh1.github.io/MedicineVerificationSystem/).

**[Bank Marketing Analysis](https://github.com/rahamasaleh1/Bankmarketinganalysis)**: I dug into 41,000 customer records to see who actually says yes to a bank's sales calls.

**[Power Plant Energy Prediction](https://github.com/rahamasaleh1/powerplantenergyprediction)**: a model that predicts a power plant's energy output, with an average error of 0.5%.

**[Subscription Leak Detector](https://github.com/rahamasaleh1/subscriptionleakdetector)**: finds the forgotten subscriptions quietly draining your account.

**[PantryPal Decision Matrix](https://pantrypal-matrix.streamlit.app/)**: I wanted a simple way to decide which feature to build first, so I made one. Change the scores and see what wins.

**[Risk Assessment Practice](https://rahamasaleh1.github.io/ktl/KTL_Risk_Assessment_Practice.html)**: a step by step walk through a risk assessment.
